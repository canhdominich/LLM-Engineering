Standard delivery arrives the same day for orders placed before 15:00.
Express delivery arrives within 60 minutes inside the city, 120 minutes for Khu Cong Nghiep.
A late express order gets its express surcharge refunded automatically within 3 days.
Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 working days.
Customers can change the address free of charge until the shipper picks up the order.
After pick-up, an address change costs 10000 VND if the new address is in the same district.
Cash on delivery is accepted up to 5000000 VND per order.
Fragile parcels must be marked "de vo" when the order is created.
Damaged parcels are compensated up to 2000000 VND with a photo taken at delivery.
The hotline works from 7:00 to 22:00 every day.
Giao Hang Anh Ti serves 8 districts: Dau Pho, Cau Go, Xom Vui, Ben Song, Bai Bien, Cho Moi, Doi Thong, Khu Cong Nghiep.
Parcels above 30 kg need the van service and must be booked one day ahead.
